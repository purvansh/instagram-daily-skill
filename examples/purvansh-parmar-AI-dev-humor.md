## voice-rules.md (excerpt)

### DO:
- Curiosity / "did you know" / "I'll teach you in 60 seconds"
- Character humor / split-screen contrast / before-vs-after observational
- Tier lists, rankings, opinions on tools (not on markets)
- Storytelling first-person ("I am sick of hearing this word X")
- Day-in-the-life, behind-the-scenes
- Hinglish closer line on most skits

### DO NOT:
- ❌ "I made $X with this template/client/tool"
  **Why:** salesy-flex tone fits the business handle, not the personal one. Personal account growth is interest-led. Confirmed user feedback: "tbh keep the content interesting and users will start coming."

- ❌ Joking about slacking on clients / missing deadlines / not delivering
  **Why:** undermines trust with prospects watching the personal account. The funnel is humor → trust → @business → DM → sale. Joking about client neglect breaks the pipeline at the trust step.

- ❌ Generic broad hashtags (#aitools, #productivity)
  **Why:** confirmed below baseline; lumps account with global SaaS spam pages.

- ❌ Talking-head text reels >25s
  **Why:** confirmed bottom-quartile retention. Specific examples that died: "5 ways to save tokens" (584v), "23 AI tools" (150v), "Cancelled subscription / OpenCode + NVIDIA" (170v at 56s).

- ❌ Off-niche serious content (history tributes, political takes)
  **Why:** "A garden that witnessed sacrifice" reel got 95 views — off-brand, off-niche, off-voice.

- ❌ Hooks from PDF categories: Finance/MLM, Fitness, Family/Parenting, Luxury Lifestyle, Dating
  **Why:** off-niche.

### SAFE targets for negative humor:
- Tool struggles (AI giving wrong output)
- Dev memes (it-works-on-my-machine)
- Founder/solo-life grind
- Platform bugs
- Prompt engineering frustration

### UNSAFE targets:
- Clients / paying customers
- Delivery quality
- Anything implying "can't ship"

### CTA rules:
- Default: "tag a [specific role] who [specific behavior]"
- Comment-keyword CTAs: NOT allowed yet (no lead magnets exist)
- Sales CTAs: business-handle-only

### Language rules:
- Primary: English
- Mix in: Hinglish punchline as closer (not the whole reel)
- Slang/curse: f*ck (asterisk softened) yes; shit no
- Religion / politics / slurs: never

### Tone calibration:
- Sarcasm: High
- Energy: Mid
- Production polish: Iphone-and-a-prayer
- Rant tolerance: Keep it tight
- Vulnerability: Surface only

## What this example demonstrates

1. **Specificity beats vagueness.** "Don't joke about clients" + the *why* > "be professional."
2. **Failed reels are data, not shame.** The bottom reels are listed by view count and used to derive the "do not" rules.
3. **Funnel rules are explicit.** Where the mention goes, what % of reels carry it, what format the mention takes.
4. **Hashtag winners and losers are listed.** The skill drops dead tags automatically because they're listed here.
5. **Voice rules ARE the brand.** Future cycles can audit the rules → audit the brief → confirm fidelity.

When you fill your own templates, aim for this level of specificity. Vague rules let the skill wander; specific rules let it ship.
