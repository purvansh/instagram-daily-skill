## Selection rules

Every hook below must be filtered against `voice-rules.md`:
- ✅ Matches your tone (curiosity / observational / storytelling / etc.)
- ❌ No "I made $X" / market-capture / sales pitches (unless this hook library is for your business handle)
- ❌ No off-niche templates (Finance/MLM/Fitness/Family/Luxury/Dating — unless that IS your niche)
- ❌ Nothing that violates the brand-image rule from `voice-rules.md`

Each row: **template (verbatim, blanks intact)** | **category** | **best format** | **pillar** | **1-line concept seed** for YOUR niche.

Format codes: **SK** = skit, **GS** = green-screen reaction, **DY** = did-you-know, **RK** = ranking, **CR** = carousel, **TH** = talking-head, **DV** = vlog cut.

## H. How to use this library day-to-day

1. **Picking tomorrow's hook:** scan rows by format you want to shoot, pick one, fill blanks.
2. **Don't over-edit the template** — these are scroll-stoppers because of their syntax.
3. **Track which row #s ship:** in `tracking/30-day-tracker.md` record `Hook ID` per reel. After 30 days, sort by performance to see which template families pull for you.
