# Voice Rules

> The skill checks every brief against this file. Be specific. Vague rules ("be funny") don't filter; specific rules ("never joke about ignoring clients") do.

## DO

What kinds of hooks, topics, formats, and phrasing are IN-bounds.

- _e.g. Curiosity / "did you know" / "I'll teach you in 60 seconds"_
- _e.g. Character humor / split-screen contrast / before-vs-after observational_
- _e.g. Tier lists, rankings, opinions on tools (not on markets)_
- _e.g. Storytelling first-person ("I am sick of hearing this word X")_
- _e.g. Day-in-the-life, behind-the-scenes_

## DO NOT

What's OUT-of-bounds for this account specifically. Reasons matter — write the WHY so the skill can apply judgment.

- ❌ _e.g. "I made $X with this template/client/tool"_  
  **Why:** salesy-flex tone fits the business handle, not the personal one. Personal account growth is interest-led.

- ❌ _e.g. Joking about slacking on clients / missing deadlines_  
  **Why:** undermines trust with prospects watching the personal account.

- ❌ _e.g. Generic broad hashtags (#aitools, #productivity)_  
  **Why:** confirmed below baseline in our data; lumps account with global SaaS spam pages.

- ❌ _e.g. Talking-head text reels >25s_  
  **Why:** confirmed bottom-quartile retention.

- ❌ _e.g. Off-niche serious content (history tributes, political takes)_  
  **Why:** off-brand, dilutes niche signal to algorithm.

- ❌ _e.g. Hooks/topics from these PDF categories: Finance/MLM, Fitness, Family/Parenting, Luxury Lifestyle, Dating_  
  **Why:** off-niche.

## SAFE targets for negative humor

When using self-deprecation or pain-target humor, the pain must point at:
- _e.g. tool struggles (AI giving wrong output)_
- _e.g. dev memes (it-works-on-my-machine)_
- _e.g. founder/solo-life grind_
- _e.g. platform bugs_

## UNSAFE targets for negative humor

The pain must NEVER point at:
- _e.g. clients / paying customers_
- _e.g. delivery quality_
- _e.g. anything implying you can't ship_

## CTA rules

- Default CTA: _e.g. "tag a [specific role] who…"_
- Comment-keyword CTAs allowed only if: lead magnet exists AND is listed in `creator-profile.md`
- Sales CTAs ("DM me to book") are: _allowed / not allowed / business-handle-only_

## Language rules

- Primary language: _e.g. English_
- Mix in: _e.g. Hinglish punchline as closer (not the whole reel)_
- Slang / curse words: _e.g. "f*ck" softened with asterisk allowed; "shit" no_
- Slurs / political topics / religion: never
